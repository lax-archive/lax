This submission collects clean Lean concepts for thirteen familiar planar graph
classes: planar, outerplanar, maximal outerplanar, grids and walls, triangles,
stars, ladders, Halin graphs, wheels, series-parallel graphs, trees, paths, and
triangulations.

The definitions follow the standard descriptions in Reinhard Diestel's
*Graph Theory* (6th edition).  A straight-line drawing is used as a compact
certificate of planarity, in accordance with Fáry's theorem.  Shape predicates
record the relevant planarity or superclass certificate; this keeps the basic
inclusion theorems fully explicit and their Lean proofs short.
