\section{Two displays and a citation of the whole work}

The treewidth of a graph never exceeds its vertex count:
% lax begin Lax261.MainBound
\begin{equation}
  \tw(G) \le |V(G)| - 1.
  \label{eq:bound}
\end{equation}
% lax end

Equality holds for complete graphs, and the two sides of \eqref{eq:bound}
agree on every clique:
% lax begin Lax261.CliqueCase
\begin{align}
  \tw(K_n) &= n - 1, \\
  |V(K_n)| - 1 &= n - 1.
\end{align}
% lax end
so the bound is tight.
